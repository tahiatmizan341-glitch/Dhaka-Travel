What to eat

Dhaka's food is rich, spiced and often slow-cooked. These restaurants are well known, and the dishes listed are the ones to order first.

Biryani · Old Dhaka

Haji Biryani

A long-running institution in the old city, famous for its rich, spiced mutton and beef biryani.

Try: Kacchi biryani with borhani (spiced yogurt drink)

Kacchi and Mughlai

Kacchi Bhai

Dhaka's well-known kacchi biryani chain. Easy to find across the city and a good first taste of the local dish.

Try: Kacchi biryani with a side of salad and borhani

Kebab and grill

Star Kabab

Dhaka's classic kebab house, serving grilled meat in a relaxed setting that suits groups and families.

Try: Seekh kebab and chicken tikka

Bengali home cooking

Bengali set meals

Many local restaurants serve a traditional thali of rice with dal, fish curry and seasonal vegetables. Ask for the daily menu.

Try: Ilish (hilsa fish) when in season, and mishti doi for dessert

Getting aroundUse ride-hailing apps for most trips. Traffic is heavy at rush hour, so plan extra time.
Eating outDhaka's street food is excellent. Choose busy stalls with high turnover and eat freshly cooked food.
Before you visitOpening hours, locations and prices change often. Check the latest details before a visit.

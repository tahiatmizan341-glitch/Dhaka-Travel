Things to explore

Start at the National Museum, then the Military Museum, then head to Dhaka's heritage, shopping malls and restaurants.

Museum

Bangladesh National Museum

Housed in the cultural heart of Shahbag, Dhaka, the Bangladesh National Museum stands as South Asia's premier multidisciplinary repository. Spanning four comprehensive floors, its extensive collections range from ancient archaeological antiquities and medieval coinage to modern art and poignant archives of the 1971 Liberation War, preserving the diverse tapestry of Bengal's natural, historical, and cultural heritage.

Military

Bangladesh Military Museum

The Bangladesh Military Museum, located in Bijoy Sarani, Dhaka, showcases the history and achievements of the Bangladesh Armed Forces. Its exhibits include military weapons, aircraft, tanks, uniforms, and historical artifacts, highlighting the nation's military heritage, the Liberation War, and its journey toward modern defence capabilities.

Heritage

Curzon Hall

Curzon Hall, a prominent example of British Raj architecture, was originally intended to be a town hall and is now home to the Faculty of Science at the University of Dhaka.

Heritage

Lalbagh Fort

A 17th-century Mughal fort with a tomb, a mosque and gardens. Go in the late afternoon for softer light.

Mughal fort, 17th century

Craft

Shopping for handicrafts

Look for Jamdani sarees, Nakshi Kantha embroidery, brassware and terracotta at craft shops and at the Aarong stores around the city. Each piece is a sign of the skill of rural artisans.

Food and culture

Jatra Biroti

If you are looking for a place to experience Bangladeshi culture, Jatra Biroti is a great choice. It brings together traditional handicrafts, clothing, cultural performances, folk music, and authentic Bangladeshi food. Visitors can enjoy popular local snacks such as fuchka and pitha, along with a variety of traditional desserts, while exploring the country's rich artistic and cultural heritage.
